Emergency management depends on knowing who is ready, where people are needed, and what has changed. When personnel records, qualifications, staffing requests, and deployment orders live in separate systems, teams have to reconstruct that picture by hand.

Usatii for emergency management brings those workflows into DART: a shared operating environment designed around readiness, response, and accountable decisions.

## Start with readiness

A staffing decision needs more than a name on a list. Qualifications, training, credentials, availability, and supporting evidence all inform whether a person is ready for an assignment. Connecting those records gives coordinators a practical foundation for reviewing candidates against incident needs.

## Connect the deployment lifecycle

The work continues after a candidate is selected. Draft orders need review and acceptance. Travel, check-in, extensions, replacements, and demobilization need to stay connected to the assignment. DART's approach treats these as parts of one operational lifecycle.

Responder accountability and equipment custody belong in that same picture. Daily checks, supervisor coverage, missed-response escalation, and equipment transfers help teams follow the people and resources supporting an incident.

## Keep decisions traceable

Federal operations require records that can explain a decision later. Scoped access, versioned rules, evidence review, correction history, and reproducible reporting make governance part of the workflow.

The aim is to reduce manual reconciliation while keeping operational facts, decisions, and field activity connected.
