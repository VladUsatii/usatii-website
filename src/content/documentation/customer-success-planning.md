## Establish a baseline

Record how the selected workflow operates today: task volumes, handling time, error rates, and the people involved. These measurements provide the basis for evaluating the pilot.

## Agree on success

Document the expected improvement, required data, responsibilities, and [acceptance criteria](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/6-2-requirements-management/) before development. Use the [Pilot Charter](/documentation#pilot-charter) to keep the test focused on an agreed operational result.

## Review the outcome

Compare the pilot with the baseline and record any limits observed during testing. Distinguish projected annual capacity from savings already realized.
