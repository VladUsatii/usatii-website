## Before your bootcamp

Identify the people who need access to the bootcamp materials, including your leadership sponsor, department representatives, and developers. Coordinate account setup with the Usatii team before the assessment. If access has not been arranged, [contact us](/contact).

Existing clients can [sign in to the client portal](/portal/login) to access their dashboard.

## Developer access

Your IT administrator should identify the existing systems, available [APIs](https://www.ibm.com/think/topics/api), and sandbox environments relevant to the pilot. Private API access and test credentials are arranged with the support team for the agreed pilot scope.

Deployment-specific API details will be provided through your account.
