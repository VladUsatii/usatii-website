## Confirm the data path

Before testing, identify what information the pilot uses, which systems process it, and whether any third-party model service is involved. The security or compliance officer should review the proposed handling of sensitive information.

## Document the agreed configuration

Record the [retention settings](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data), regional endpoints, and provider agreements for your deployment. Refer to [Protecting your company’s information](/documentation/bootcamp#protecting-your-companys-information) for the bootcamp’s current overview.

Confirm the configuration for your pilot with the Usatii team before sharing data.
