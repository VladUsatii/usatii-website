## Involve the people doing the work

Invite employees who perform the affected workflows to test the pilot with the FDE team. Use their feedback to identify exceptions, clarify responsibilities, and improve the handoff before production work begins.

## Prepare the handoff

[Change management](https://www.prosci.com/blog/definition-of-change-management) helps teams prepare for a new way of working. Leadership should explain the proposed changes, make room for employee concerns, and identify who needs training. The bootcamp gives staff time to understand how the solution processes information and fits into their daily work.

Start with the [bootcamp overview](/documentation#bootcamp-overview).
